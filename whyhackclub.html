Why choose Hack Club?

Learn a useful new hobby!

Is what I would say if I wanted to summarize my points.


The main idea and reasoning I have behind my decision when someone asks me, "Why Hack Club?" is that I enjoy coding.

But if you wanted my real answer, it's because I believe it's great to have polyhobbies, as in many hobbies!! >< We live in a society where coding and scripting is gaining a higher prevalence than it ever held before. I mean yes, coding has always been a vital part of human development, it's the FUTURE of humanity. That only adds to my point, however. It's good to have a fun hobby, but it's even more valuable when the hobby is useful in current society. Or even, future and past society!
